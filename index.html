Video Studio

A personal publishing tool that the owner of this site uses to produce and schedule videos for their own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Writes short story videos (narration, captions and gameplay background) on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads and schedules those videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Posts one comment from the channel on its own videos to start discussion.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views and average percentage watched) to learn which videos viewers enjoy.

Who can use it

Video Studio is not a public service. It has no sign-ups and no other users. The only Google account that signs in is the owner's, to manage the owner's own channel.
